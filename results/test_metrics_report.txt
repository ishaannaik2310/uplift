  qini_auc_score (sklift norm.)               N/A      -0.005064

  auuc_normalized (minus random)       -44.583053            N/A
  uplift_auc_score (sklift norm.)             N/A      -0.003232

  NORMALIZATION EXPLANATION:
  - Our qini_coefficient:
      = AUC(Q curve) - Q(1)/2
      Subtracts the random diagonal. NOT divided by a perfect model.
      Raw units: cumulative incremental outcomes (same scale as Q values).
      Divided by N gives incremental outcomes per customer.

  - sklift.qini_auc_score:
      = (AUC_actual - AUC_baseline) / (AUC_perfect - AUC_baseline)
      x-axis in counts (range [0, N]), making AUC scale with N.
      Normalizes by the PERFECT model, producing a score in ~[0, 1].
      Not directly comparable to our coefficient without perfect-model AUC.

  - Our auuc / auuc_normalized:
      = trapz(U(k) dk) with x in fraction [0,1].
      _normalized subtracts U(1)/2 (random diagonal area).
      NOT divided by perfect model AUC.

  - sklift.uplift_auc_score:
      = (AUC_actual - AUC_baseline) / (AUC_perfect - AUC_baseline)
      Normalizes by the perfect uplift curve. Dimensionless ratio.
      Cannot be directly compared to our raw AUUC values.

  CONCLUSION: Our metrics and sklift metrics measure the same shape of curve
  (formula is identical) but differ in x-axis scale (fraction vs count) and
  in normalization (absolute area vs ratio to perfect model). The curve
  point-by-point comparison after x-axis rescaling shows agreement.
